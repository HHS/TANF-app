Reporting Due Dates

This page provides the quarterly reporting deadlines for reports submitted through the TANF Data Portal (TDP).

Use the tables below to identify the submission due date for each reporting period.

Submission deadlines are established by the Office of Family Assistance (OFA). If a deadline changes, this page (as well as deadlines listed in TDP) will be updated to reflect the current reporting schedule. If you have questions about reporting requirements, submission deadlines, or your reporting obligations, please contact the TANF Data Team at tanfdata@acf.hhs.gov.
